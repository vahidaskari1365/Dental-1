   311→## Glossary
   312→
   313→| Abbreviation | Meaning             |
   314→| ------------ | ------------------- |
   315→| **RPM**      | Requests per minute |
   316→| **RPD**      | Requests per day    |
   317→| **TPM**      | Tokens per minute   |
   318→| **TPD**      | Tokens per day      |
   319→| **RPS**      | Requests per second |
